This Place is Our Archive

Session: Hazards, Risks, & Disasters | San Francisco, CA

We worked with residents in the West Side of Port Arthur to preserve and map historically Black locations that now primarily exist in their memories after decades of refinery expansion and major storm events have destroyed their community. Using a combination of Port Arthur oral histories, Google MyMaps, ArcGIS StoryMaps, and video editing software, we created a visual (and audio) depiction of how Black relationships to place and environment developed in the West Side, and how the oil refineries and climate change complicate them. Overall, this project aims to preserve the longstanding Black relationships to the places built and destroyed, but not forgotten, in the West Side of Port Arthur.
